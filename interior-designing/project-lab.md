# Interior Designing — Hands-On Project Lab

> Design a full floor plan and mood board for a small studio apartment.

## Objective

Practice space planning, traffic flow, and material selection under real constraints (a small footprint).

## Prerequisites

- Any diagramming tool (even paper) for the floor plan
- A defined room size, e.g. 350 sq ft

## Steps

1. Sketch the room's fixed elements (windows, doors, plumbing) to scale.
2. Zone the space into sleep, work, and living areas without physical walls.
3. Draw furniture placement that keeps a clear traffic path of at least 30 inches.
4. Choose a color palette (2-3 colors) and justify it in terms of making the small space feel larger.
5. Build a mood board (fabric/material/color references) that matches the palette.
6. List a lighting plan: ambient, task, and accent lighting for each zone.

## Deliverable

A to-scale floor plan, a mood board, and a written lighting plan.

## Stretch goals

- Create a simple 3D mockup of the space using a free tool (e.g. SketchUp free tier).
